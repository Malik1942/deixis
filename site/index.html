Agents don't understand this. Deixis does.

One gesture. Three seconds.

Deixis lives in the menu bar and in a small glass ball at the edge of the screen. It appears, is used, and is gone.

1

Point

Double-tap ⌃ or click the ball. Hover to see the highlight and the label. Click the element.

2

Note

Type what should change. Enter captures. Esc cancels with nothing written.

3

Paste

The clipboard holds a Markdown payload and the image path. Paste into Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex.

What your agent receives

Role, label, accessibility identifier, frame, and ancestry, plus a cropped PNG. Enough to find the right file on the first try.

When there is no element

Figma, games, and custom-drawn views expose little. Deixis steps down a ladder and tells the agent which rung it reached.

See what changed

After the agent's edit, choose Capture after. Deixis finds the same element again in the running app, captures it, records the git facts, and opens the two images side by side with one zoom.

Deixis never commits, never installs hooks, never talks to the network.

Settings that look like Settings

The one place Deixis is a window. The hotkey is recorded by pressing it. Everything else has a default that works on first launch.

Private by design

Two permissions, both explained

Accessibility reads what is under your cursor and listens for the hotkey. Screen Recording captures the pixels. There is no third prompt.

Nothing leaves the machine

No network, no telemetry, no accounts. Captures live in your Pictures folder, tagged in Finder, and move to the Trash after 30 days unless pinned.

Signed and notarized

Every build is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Distributed as a dmg, not through the App Store.

Lightweight

No Dock icon. Idle memory under 30 MB. Nothing pulses, bounces, or waits for you.

Known limitations

  • Element quality follows the app's accessibility. SwiftUI, AppKit, and the iOS Simulator work well. Electron and Chromium apps build their tree on first contact, so the first hover can take about half a second to sharpen.
  • Custom-drawn UIs expose little. Figma, games, and canvases usually reach the bottom of the ladder. The payload says so.
  • Hover picks the smallest real control and sticks to it across padding. Press Option to step to the parent.
